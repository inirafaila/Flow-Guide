# Product vision

## North star

Become the **default, trusted companion** for non-Armenian newcomers navigating **first 90 days** (and beyond) — through **clear sequencing**, **honest uncertainty**, and **cited sources**, not generic blogs.

## Principles

1. **Reduce anxiety** — calm, scannable, action-first UI.
2. **Right sequence over more content** — fewer, right steps beat encyclopedic noise.
3. **Trust via transparency** — sources, dates, and limits of advice visible.
4. **Start free, earn account** — guest-first; account when value is obvious.
5. **Grow from guide to platform** — MVP is narrow but architecture allows services and community later without rewriting IA fundamentals.

## Non-vision (MVP)

Not a job marketplace, not a full map stack, not community Q&A as core — see `PRD_MVP.md` non-goals.

## Horizon

Post-MVP: deeper personalization, optional services, richer search, partner tools — gated by `ROADMAP_MASTER.md` and post-launch protocol.
