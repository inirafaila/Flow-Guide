# Project overview

**Flow-guide** is a **web application** helping **newcomers to Armenia** navigate **ordered steps** (residency, housing, work, payments, transport, daily ops) with **trustworthy, source-backed** content and a **personal dashboard**.

## Problem

Information is scattered, often outdated or contradictory, and rarely tailored to **sequence** and **early survival constraints** (e.g. before local bank or full residency).

## Solution shape (MVP)

Hybrid **Home**, **onboarding**, **dashboard**, **guide** pages, **search**, **updates**, **checklist** with **guest-first** access and **account-later** for persistence and services.

## Audience

Primary: **newcomers** (multiple languages). Secondary later: partners, employers — out of MVP core.

## Differentiators

- Scenario and **next-action** orientation.
- **Sources** and verification discipline for high-risk topics.
- **Mobile-first**, calm UX (see `DESIGN_DIRECTION.md`).
