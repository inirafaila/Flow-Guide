# Success metrics

Define baselines after launch; instrument per `ANALYTICS_PLAN.md`.

## North-star (candidate)

- **Activation:** % guests who start onboarding or open a guide within first session.
- **Progress:** % users who complete **N** checklist items or view **next best action** follow-through (define N in analytics spec).

## Quality & trust

- **Source engagement:** clicks on sources / expands of source blocks on sensitive pages.
- **Stale flags:** count of content pieces past verification SLA (internal).

## Growth

- **Return within 7 days** (guest or account).
- **Account conversion** after value moment (dashboard save, checklist sync).

## Guardrails

- Bounce rate on Home (segment by traffic source).
- Error rate on search and dashboard loads.

Review quarterly; tie changes to `POST_LAUNCH_CHANGE_PROTOCOL.md`.
