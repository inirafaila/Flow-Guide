# Content template rules

For writers and implementers of guide/hub content.

## Structure

- **Title** + **summary** (one to two lines).
- **When this applies** / prerequisites when relevant.
- **Steps** or **sections** with clear headings (scannable).
- **Sources** block mandatory for residency, financial, legal-adjacent claims.
- **Related links** to hubs or next actions.

## Voice

- Calm, direct, **newcomer-first**; avoid jargon or explain once.
- Prefer **imperative** for actions (“Bring your passport,” not “One might consider…”).

## Staleness

- Tag content with **last verified** in CMS; see `DATA_STALENESS.md`.

## Localization

- **English slug**; localized titles and body in CMS per locale.
- RTL layout considerations for FA.

## What not to do

- No uncited numeric legal/fee claims.
- No promise of outcomes (“guaranteed visa”) — describe process and point to sources.
