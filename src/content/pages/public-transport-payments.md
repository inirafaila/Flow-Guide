## Quick summary

**Public transport** in **Yerevan** is **generally affordable** compared with many European cities, but **payment rules**—**metro vs. bus**, **cash vs. card**, **plastic vs. mobile**—**shift over time**. Use this page for **orientation**; **verify fares and pass types locally** before you rely on them for commuting.

## Metro and bus payment basics

- **Metro** — stations usually have **ticket offices or machines**; some periods have used **reloadable smart cards**—**check current signage** at entry.
- **Buses (including marshrutkas in some routes)** — payment may be **cash to the driver**, **tap card**, or **other**—**rules differ by operator and route**; watch what locals do and ask if unsure.
- **Regional buses / intercity** — often **cash**; **confirm price before boarding** when possible.

**Do not treat any informal “typical fare” as a promise**—operator and city policy change.

## Cards and top-up (generic)

Many cities use **stored-value cards** that you **reload** at **metro kiosks**, **terminals**, or **partner shops**. Card **names, deposit rules, and where you can top up** change—**read the card packaging** or ask at a **metro ticket window**.

If you use **mobile ticketing** in an app, **download from official stores** and **avoid sharing OTPs** with strangers “helping” you install software.

**Topping up phone data** for navigation apps is often easier via [Payment terminals](/payments/terminals) if you are not on Wi‑Fi.

## Tips

- **Carry small AMD notes** for cash routes; drivers may not break large bills smoothly.
- **Validate tickets** if the system requires tapping in/out—**fines and inspection rules** depend on local enforcement; **confirm locally**.
- **Peak hours** are crowded; **keep valuables secure**.
- For **airport arrival** and first rides, see [Airport to city](/newcomer/airport-to-city).
