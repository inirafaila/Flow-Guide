## Quick summary

**Yandex Go** is widely used in Armenia for **taxi-style rides and delivery**. To work as a driver or courier you typically install a **partner (Pro)** app, complete verification, and connect a **payout method**. Fees, bonuses, and rules **change often**—treat numbers below as **orientation only** and **confirm in the app** before you rely on them.

## Requirements

- **Smartphone** — Android or iOS; partner apps are usually separate from the passenger Yandex Go app.
- **SIM / number** — a **working local number** is typically required for codes and support.
- **ID and eligibility** — the partner flow usually asks for identity documents; requirements may depend on **citizenship/residency** and **vehicle type** (for driving).
- **Payments** — you need a way to receive earnings (often **bank card or account**); see [Payments](/payments) for context on cards and transfers in Armenia.
- **Vehicle** (for ride-share) — insurance, registration, and any local checks the platform mandates—**confirm in the partner app**.

## Step-by-step setup (high level)

1. **Install the correct partner app** — search the store for the current **Yandex Pro** (or equivalent partner) listing for your role (driver vs. courier); names and bundles can change by region.
2. **Create or sign in to a Yandex account** — follow in-app prompts; keep **two-factor / recovery options** somewhere safe.
3. **Choose your work mode** — delivery-only vs. rides, if offered; some cities emphasize one mode.
4. **Upload documents** — passport/ID, driver license if applicable, vehicle papers if you drive; wait for **verification** (timing varies).
5. **Add payout details** — link a **card or bank account** that matches platform rules; small test payouts are common.
6. **Go online** — start only when the app shows you as **approved**; follow **local traffic and platform safety** rules.

If a step fails, support channels and document lists change—**use the in-app help** for authoritative requirements.

## Earnings and costs

- **Gross vs. net** — platforms show **gross** trip or task pay; **commission**, insurance, fuel, and vehicle wear reduce what you keep.
- **Typical framing** — newcomers often see **variable** daily or weekly totals depending on hours, zone, and demand; **no** reliable universal number fits everyone.
- **Surge and bonuses** — may appear in-app; **always read terms**—bonuses can have minimum trips or time windows.

**Confirm commission percentages, minimum payouts, and any subscription or rental fees in the current partner agreement.**

## Tips

- **Start in daylight** in an area you know; learn navigation and the app’s safety tools before long shifts.
- **Keep documents current** — expired license or insurance can deactivate your account without notice.
- **Track expenses** if you drive—fuel and maintenance matter for real take-home.
