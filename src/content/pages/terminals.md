## Quick summary

**Payment terminals** are self-service kiosks—often bright yellow or branded—found in **shops, malls, metro stations, and on busy streets**. They let you **pay bills, top up mobile data, and sometimes buy transport or wallet top-ups** without a bank branch visit. Networks and menus **change**; use this page for orientation, then **read the screen** each time you pay.

## What terminals are used for

Typical uses (availability varies by machine and network):

- **Mobile phone balance** and prepaid data packages.
- **Utility and service bills** — electricity, water, internet, or bundled providers (exact menu labels vary).
- **Wallet or e-money top-ups** — some terminals link to widely used local wallet brands.
- **Other billers** — insurance, TV, or municipal-style payments sometimes appear in menus—**select carefully** and double-check the recipient name.

**Fees** (if any) are usually shown **before** you confirm; if something looks wrong, **cancel** and try another terminal or channel.

## How to use (high level)

1. **Choose language** if offered—English is not always available; ask for help in shops if needed.
2. **Pick the category** (mobile, utilities, etc.), then the **provider** from the list.
3. **Enter the account or phone number** the biller gave you—**one wrong digit** can send money to the wrong place.
4. **Enter the amount** or pick a package; the screen should show **any service fee** before payment.
5. **Pay** — often **cash (AMD)**; some locations accept **cards**—**confirm locally** what the machine accepts.
6. **Take the receipt** and keep it until the payment posts (can take **minutes to a day** depending on the biller).

If the machine eats cash without printing, note **location, time, and terminal ID** and contact the **network operator** or shop staff—**this is not legal advice**; procedures vary.

## iDram, Telcell, and similar

**iDram** and **Telcell** are **widely used** Armenian payment/wallet ecosystems—often reachable via **apps** as well as terminal-style flows in shops. **Commission, limits, and menus change**; do not rely on informal fee tables. For app-first setup, see [Essential apps](/daily-life/essential-apps). This guide focuses on **physical terminals**; the same brands may appear in different channels with **different** pricing.

## Safety and receipts

- **Use machines in well-lit, busy places** when you can; shield your PIN if paying by card.
- **Check the payee name** on the confirmation screen matches your bill.
- **Keep receipts** for disputes or landlord proof of payment—screenshots help if the app sends confirmations.
- **Carry small AMD notes**—not every kiosk gives change for large bills smoothly.

Opening a **local bank account** for cards and transfers usually needs a **social card** and related steps—see [Social card](/documents/social-card).
