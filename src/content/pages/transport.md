Getting around **Yerevan** is usually cheap by European standards: **ride-share apps**, **metro**, and **buses** cover most daily needs. For your **first ride from the airport**, apps and fixed-price desks are the main options—see the dedicated guide below.

## Top tasks

- [Public transport payments](/transport/public-transport-payments) — Tickets, cards, and paying for buses and metro.
- [Airport to city](/newcomer/airport-to-city) — Zvartnots to Yerevan—taxis, apps, and typical costs.

## What to know first

- **GG** and **Yandex Go** are widely used for taxis; prices are **typically** low compared to Western Europe—**confirm in the app** before you ride.
- **Metro and buses** work for many commutes; routes and payment rules can change—check current info locally.
- Fares are **generally affordable**, but **traffic and routing** still take getting used to.
- **Addresses** can be confusing (building numbers, courtyards)—use a map pin or Armenian text when possible.
