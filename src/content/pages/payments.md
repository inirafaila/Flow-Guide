Day-to-day life in Armenia runs heavily on **cash** and **payment terminals**—even if you do not have a local bank account yet. Many newcomers rely on **in-person top-ups, terminal networks, and mobile wallets** before they complete ID steps needed for full banking.

## Top tasks

- [Payment terminals](/payments/terminals) — Finding and using terminals for bills and phone top-ups.
- [Service payments](/payments/service-payments) — Paying utilities and common services.

## What to know first

- **Cash is still central**—keep some **AMD** on hand; not every small shop or driver takes cards.
- **Terminal networks** are everywhere in cities; you can pay many bills and top up phones without a bank login.
- **iDram**, **Telcell**, and similar apps are widely used once you have a number and (often) an account—see [Essential apps](/daily-life/essential-apps).
- A **local bank account** usually needs a **social card** and address registration first—plan for a **no-bank-account** phase.
