## Quick summary

In your **first seven days**, aim to **get connected**, **stabilize housing** (or extend a short stay), and **start the paperwork chain**—usually **address registration** first, then planning for **social card** and **payments**. Pace yourself: some steps take **several business days**, not one afternoon.

## Day-by-day priorities

### Days 1–2

Settle in, get a **SIM**, buy **AMD**, and orient yourself—follow [Day one](/newcomer/day-one) for arrival-day basics.

### Days 3–4

If you need a longer-term place, **start housing search** seriously—see [Housing](/housing). When you have a lawful address, begin **address registration**—[Address registration](/documents/address-registration).

### Days 5–7

Apply for or schedule **social card** after address registration is moving—[Social card](/documents/social-card). Explore **payment setup** ([Payment terminals](/payments/terminals)) and skim **income options** ([Quick income](/work/quick-income)) if you need cash flow soon.

## Don't panic about

- **Residency and long-term status** — Often takes weeks or months of coordinated steps; focus on the next official requirement, not the whole path at once.
- **Bank account** — Usually comes **after** social card for many people; a no-bank-account phase is normal.
- **Formal employment** — May wait on IDs and contracts; informal or app-based work is common in the meantime—always **confirm legality** for your situation.
