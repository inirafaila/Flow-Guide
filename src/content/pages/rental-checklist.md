## Quick summary

Use this list to **reduce surprises** before and after you move. It is **not** a substitute for reading your **contract**, talking to **utilities providers**, or getting **professional advice** when stakes are high. Mark what matters most to **you**—families, remote workers, and pet owners will weight items differently.

## Before you visit

- **Clarify rent terms** — gross vs net utilities, **what furniture stays**, and **minimum lease length** in messages before you travel across town.
- **Check commute reality** — map pins can be approximate; confirm **building entrance** and **stairs/elevator**.
- **Budget extras** — agency fee (if any), **deposit**, first months, and **cash vs transfer** preferences.
- **Bring** — notebook, **charged phone** for photos, **torch** for dark corners if viewing in evening, and a **friend or translator** if negotiations are not in your strongest language.

## During viewing

- **Water pressure** — run taps and shower; look for **leaks** under sinks.
- **Heating and cooling** — how the flat is **heated** (gas boiler, central, AC units); **test remotes** if included.
- **Electricity** — enough outlets where you work? **Circuit breaker** location?
- **Windows and noise** — street vs courtyard; **double glazing** matters on busy roads.
- **Hot water** — **tank vs combi**; recovery time if multiple people shower.
- **Internet** — can you see **where fiber enters** or confirm **ISP** options with neighbors or building?
- **Neighbors and building** — **smell**, **pets**, **construction** nearby; **garbage** area and **stairs** cleanliness.
- **Security** — main door, **intercom**, mailbox; **who has keys** besides you.

Take **dated photos/video** of **walls, floors, appliances, meters**—**before** you move things in.

## Before signing

- **Written contract** in a language you can read—or **get a translation** of key clauses before you commit.
- **Parties match** — signer name vs property documents you are shown (even at high level); **mismatches** need explanation.
- **Deposit and rent** — **due dates**, **methods**, **receipts**; **refund conditions** for deposit.
- **Utilities** — which meters are **in your name** vs landlord’s; **opening balances** if you take over accounts.
- **Repairs** — who fixes **what** and **how fast**; **do not rely** on verbal-only promises for big items.
- **Notice to vacate** — **how many months’ notice**, **penalties**, **inventory return**.

**This is not a legal checklist**—it is a **practical** one.

## After move-in basics

- **Meter readings** — photo **day one** where relevant; align with **bills or handover sheet** if provided.
- **Service payments** — set up **terminal, bank, or app** payments you will actually use — [Service payments](/payments/service-payments).
- **Address registration** — when you are ready for the official step, follow [Address registration](/documents/address-registration); **timing** depends on your stay plans and lawful housing proof.
- **Small fixes** — **document** anything promised for “after move-in” with **date and channel** (message thread).
