## Quick summary

Your phone becomes **navigation, wallet, and social lifeline** quickly. This page lists **categories** and **example app names** people commonly mention—**not endorsements**. **Uninstall** what you do not use; **permissions** (contacts, location, camera) should match what you expect.

## Connectivity (SIM / data context)

- You usually need a **local SIM or eSIM** for **reliable SMS** (bank and app codes) and **affordable mobile data**.
- **Top up** via shops, [Payment terminals](/payments/terminals), or carrier apps—**plans and bundles change**.
- **Wi‑Fi** is common in cafés; **do not rely** on it for **2FA** when you are out.

## Messaging

- **Global:** WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal—**usage varies** by community; **Telegram** is very common in regional professional and housing chats.
- **Video calls** — WhatsApp, FaceTime (where available), Google Meet—**pick what your contacts actually use**.

Treat **group links** from strangers like **open doors**—**verify** before sharing personal documents.

## Maps and transport

- **Maps** — **Google Maps** and **Yandex Maps** both see use; **traffic and pedestrian routing** quality can differ by area—**compare** for your neighborhood.
- **Ride-share / taxi** — names often cited include **Yandex Go** and **GG**; **fares** are **in-app**—**confirm before you ride**.
- **Public transport** — see [Transport](/transport) and [Public transport payments](/transport/public-transport-payments); **metro/bus apps** (if any) change—**check store listings** locally.

## Food and delivery

- **Delivery marketplaces** — international and local brands rotate promotions; **service fees** appear at checkout—**read totals**.
- **Allergies and dietary needs** — **menu accuracy** varies; when in doubt, **call the restaurant**.

## Payments and wallets

- **Bank apps** — once you have an account, your **bank’s app** is usually central for transfers.
- **Wallets / bill pay** — **iDram**, **Telcell**, and similar brands appear often for **top-ups and bills**—**commissions and limits** change; see also [Payment terminals](/payments/terminals).

**Never share** OTPs, card PINs, or **remote-desktop** access with “support” who cold-call you.

## Privacy and account safety (brief)

- Use **official app stores**; **avoid sideloaded** APKs promising “free premium.”
- **Separate email** for banking if you can; enable **2FA** where offered.
- **Lock screen** and **device encryption** on; **remote wipe** if your phone is stolen.
- **Screenshots** of chats can be **evidence**—but also **metadata**; think before sharing externally.
