## Quick summary

Some job listings refer to **live-dealer**, **game presenter**, or **streaming casino-style** work: hosting table games on camera for online players. This page gives **neutral orientation** only. **We are not recruiting**, **not promising income**, and **not giving legal advice**. Before you engage, you must **verify legality, work authorization, tax obligations, and contract terms** for your situation in Armenia.

## What this usually refers to

- **Live-dealer studios** — staff operate card or wheel games from a studio; video is streamed to remote players.
- **Presenter-style roles** — similar on-camera work with strict scripts and compliance rules set by the operator.
- **Agency vs. direct hire** — some markets use staffing agencies; contracts and protections differ.

Descriptions on job boards **vary in accuracy**; treat them as leads to investigate, not facts.

## What to verify before engaging

- **Legality and licensing** — which operators may employ people in Armenia for which markets is a **regulatory** question; **confirm with official sources** or qualified counsel—not forum posts alone.
- **Right to work** — your **residency and work permission** must match the role; immigration rules are fact-specific — see [Documents](/documents).
- **Tax and social contributions** — how pay is classified (employee vs. contractor), what is withheld, and what you must declare—**get clarity in writing** and consider professional tax advice.
- **Contract terms** — base pay vs. tips or performance metrics, probation, exclusivity, NDAs, and **grounds for termination**; **read before you sign**.
- **Work conditions** — shifts, breaks, training, and workplace policies; if something is only promised verbally, **ask for it in the contract or addendum**.

## Risks

- **Regulatory change** — operators and target markets can shift; roles can end with short notice.
- **Reputation and mobility** — niche experience may not transfer to other industries.
- **Payment disputes** — cross-border pay structures can complicate complaints; **know the legal entity** paying you.
- **Misleading ads** — high advertised pay may omit deductions or hour requirements.

**Do not rely on this page for whether a specific employer or offer is lawful or suitable.**
