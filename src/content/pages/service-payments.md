## Quick summary

**Service payments** here means **regular bills and subscriptions** newcomers usually hit after moving in: **utilities, internet, mobile, and sometimes building or shared costs**. You can often pay through **terminals**, **bank apps**, or **provider websites**—but **account codes and menus vary**. Avoid **hard-coding** amounts from memory; **confirm balances** each cycle.

## Common bill types newcomers hit

- **Electricity and water** — often tied to a **meter or subscriber number** on your paperwork.
- **Fixed internet / TV / bundled telecom** — contract or account ID required.
- **Mobile prepaid/postpaid** — phone number-based top-ups are common.
- **Building or shared fees** — sometimes informal; **get written terms** from landlord or building admin when possible.

Exact **labels and required fields** depend on the **provider and your contract**—not universal.

## Channels

- **Payment terminals** — walk-up, cash-heavy; good when you lack full banking yet—see [Payment terminals](/payments/terminals).
- **Bank web/mobile** — once you have a **local account** and biller is supported in the bank’s directory.
- **Provider apps and sites** — some utilities push **direct debit** or in-app payment; **availability varies**.
- **In-person at provider offices** — slower but can help when account setup is unclear.

**Fees and posting times** differ by channel—**read the confirmation screen** before you approve.

## What to have ready

Gather **before** you pay (examples—your situation may differ):

- **Account or contract numbers** exactly as printed (spelling, leading zeros).
- **Passport or ID** if an office requires it for first-time setup.
- **Enough AMD** for cash channels, or a **working card** if paying at a terminal or bank that accepts cards.
- **Landlord or agent contact** if a bill is in someone else’s name—**clarify who pays** to avoid double payment.

If you are unsure which code to enter, **pause** and confirm with the **bill issuer** or a **trusted local contact**—wrong digits are a common mistake.
