Everything about legal paperwork and residency status in Armenia — from address registration to temporary residency.

## Top tasks

- [Address registration](/documents/address-registration) — Register your address; usually the first paperwork step.
- [Stay calculator](/documents/stay-calculator) — Check how many days you have left in the rolling 180-day window.
- [Social card](/documents/social-card) — Your key ID document for services and banking.
- [Temporary residency](/documents/temporary-residency) — Longer-term legal status after initial registration.

## Common order

Most newcomers follow this general sequence:

1. **Address registration** — as soon as you have a rental agreement or landlord consent.
2. **Social card** — requires address registration; needed for banking and many services.
3. **Temporary residency** — if you plan to stay longer-term; builds on address registration.
