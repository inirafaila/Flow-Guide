Settling in means **apps**, **messengers**, and **local services**: food delivery, ride-share, phone top-ups, and staying in touch. This hub focuses on **practical day-to-day setup**—not tourism listings.

## Top tasks

- [Essential apps](/daily-life/essential-apps) — SIM, messaging, delivery, taxis, and payments.

## What to know first

- **English** is common in central **Yerevan** cafés, tech circles, and many services—**less so** in smaller shops or outside the center.
- **Delivery apps** (food, groceries) are active in urban areas; coverage varies by address.
- **WhatsApp** and **Telegram** are standard for landlords, drivers, and many businesses.
- **Russian** still helps in many everyday interactions; **Armenian** is appreciated if you learn basics.
