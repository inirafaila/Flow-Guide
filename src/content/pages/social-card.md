## Quick summary

The **social card** (also described as a **public services number** or similar national identifier) is Armenia’s standard way to assign you a **unique identification number** for public services—similar in role to a **Social Security Number** in other countries. Most newcomers who live and work in Armenia need it for **banking, employment, tax registration, healthcare enrollment**, and many **government or utility** interactions. After a complete application, processing is **often several business days to a few weeks**, but timing **depends on the office and your case**—**confirm locally**.

## Who needs this

- Almost all non-citizens planning to **work, open a bank account, or access services** in Armenia.
- Usually the **second major paperwork step** after address registration.

## Prerequisites

- **Address registration** must be completed first in most cases.
- If you haven't registered your address yet, start there: [Address registration](/documents/address-registration).

## What you need

- **Passport** (original and copy).
- **Address registration confirmation** (stamp or certificate).
- **Completed application form** (available at the office).
- **[Fee, if applicable]** — confirm current fee before you go.

## Steps

1. **Complete address registration** first — [guide here](/documents/address-registration).
2. **Gather required documents** — passport, address registration proof, copies.
3. **Visit the territorial service center** (or the responsible office in your area).
4. **Submit your application** — staff will review; processing time is **[typically several business days to a few weeks — confirm locally]**.
5. **Receive your social card** — you'll get a card or number that you'll use for banking, employment, and government services.

- **Fee:** **[confirm at office]** — amounts may change.

## Warnings

- Do **not** skip address registration — most offices will require it before processing your social card application.
- The social card number is **sensitive personal information** — keep it secure.
- Requirements **may differ** depending on your nationality and residency status.
- If you plan to open a bank account, the social card is usually required — plan accordingly.
