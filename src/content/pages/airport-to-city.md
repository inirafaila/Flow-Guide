## Quick summary

**Zvartnots International Airport** is **around 12–15 km** from central Yerevan—**typically** **20–40 minutes** by car depending on traffic. For most newcomers, **GG** or **Yandex Go** offers **predictable pricing** in the **low thousands of AMD** range; airport desks quote **higher fixed prices** but can be simpler if you have no data yet. **Confirm** fares in the app or at the desk—**numbers below are approximate**.

## Your options

### GG Taxi / Yandex Go app

Often the **cheapest predictable option** for many travelers—**typically around 2,000–3,500 AMD** to central Yerevan, **subject to surge and time of day**. You need **mobile data** or **airport Wi‑Fi** to download the app and request a ride.

### Airport taxi desk

**Fixed-price** rides from official counters—**often around 5,000–8,000 AMD** to central areas in community reports; **confirm the price before you pay**. More expensive than apps for many routes, but straightforward if you prefer not to negotiate on the curb.

### Random taxi drivers

Approaching drivers in the arrivals area may lead to **higher quotes** or confusion. If you use them, **agree on the full price in AMD** before you enter the car—or prefer apps or the desk.

### Airport shuttle / bus

Public or shared options **may** exist with **lower fares** but **less frequent** service and less door-to-door convenience—**check current schedules locally**; not always practical with heavy luggage or late arrivals.

## Step by step

1. **Connect to airport Wi‑Fi** (or use roaming) so you can install or open a ride app.
2. **Download GG or Yandex Go** if you do not have them yet; create/log into an account if required.
3. **Order a ride** to your accommodation—drop a **map pin** or paste the address; Armenian text helps drivers.
4. **Have your address ready**—screenshot, map link, or written Armenian if needed.
5. **Pay** as the app allows—many rides accept **card in-app** or **cash**; **confirm** in the app before you book.

## Tips

- Keep **some AMD cash** even if you pay in-app—small notes help for tips or if the app fails.
- **Screenshot** your destination and any building entry instructions.
- **Late night** may mean **longer waits** or **fewer drivers**—the desk or a pre-arranged pickup can be worth it if you are exhausted.
- If you do not have data immediately, **airport Wi‑Fi** first, then SIM setup—see [Day one](/newcomer/day-one).

## What may vary

Fares **change** with **traffic, time of day, fuel prices, and app surge pricing**. Seasonal tourism and events can also affect availability—**treat any number as a rough guide**, not a guarantee.
