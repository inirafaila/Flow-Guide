Income and work in Armenia often start informally: many newcomers pick up **delivery, ride-share, or freelance** gigs before they land formal employment. Cash-in-hand and app-based work are common while you sort out longer-term plans—especially if residency paperwork is still in progress.

## What to know first

- **Freelance and app-based work** are widespread; formal payroll jobs exist but often expect **address registration, social card, and sometimes residency** to be further along.
- **Cash still matters** in day-to-day life—understand how you get paid and how you move money ([Payments](/payments)).
- **Russian** is often useful with clients, landlords, and service providers, even if you operate in English online.
- **Formal employment** usually lines up with **residency and ID paperwork**—see [Documents](/documents) before assuming you can sign a standard contract.
