Often **yes** for daily life, especially with **cash (AMD)** and **payment terminals** for utilities and many services. Card acceptance **varies** by shop, taxi, and market—do not assume every place takes foreign cards or apps.

Many newcomers use:

- **Cash** for transport, markets, and smaller vendors.
- **TelCell / EasyPay-style terminals** for bills once you know the account or contract numbers.
- **International cards or apps** where accepted—availability **depends on the merchant**.

For field-tested patterns (not legal or fee advice), see [Payment terminals](/payments/terminals) and [Service payments](/payments/service-payments).
