“Quick income” usually means **short setup time**, not guaranteed high pay. Common newcomer paths include **ride-hail / delivery platforms**, **freelance or remote work** (if your visa/status allows), and **service or hospitality roles**—each has different **document, tax, and contract** implications.

Before you start:

- Confirm your **legal right to work** for your nationality and current status.
- Understand **payout method** (cash, foreign account, local account later).
- Treat informal “easy money” offers with caution.
