Take your time before paying a **deposit** or signing. Visit the unit if possible, confirm **what is included in rent** (utilities, furniture, repairs), and get key terms **in writing** even for short stays.

Useful habits:

- Compare **owner-direct vs agency** listings—you pay for different risks and services.
- Walk through a **checklist** (meter readings, locks, heating, internet) before handover.
- Avoid sending large sums to strangers without a clear contract or escrow-style protection.

Deeper guides: [Owner vs agency](/housing/owner-vs-agency) and [Rental checklist](/housing/rental-checklist). For formal housing requests, the app has a separate flow—not covered in this FAQ.
