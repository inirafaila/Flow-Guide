Not having **temporary residency** (or equivalent status) yet is common in the first weeks. You can often still handle **basics**—SIM, cash, housing search, some income paths—but **official procedures and banking** usually depend on your **actual situation and nationality**.

A practical approach:

1. Stabilize **housing and documents you already have** (passport, entry stamp, rental contract if any).
2. Understand whether you need **address registration** before other steps—rules and urgency **vary by case**.
3. Plan **residency or permit routes** with official sources, not social-media summaries.

Use the [Documents](/documents) hub for order-of-operations, then the dedicated guides for [Address registration](/documents/address-registration) and [Temporary residency](/documents/temporary-residency). Procedures and fees **change**—verify on official portals linked from those guides.
