Yes—most newcomers can get a **prepaid SIM** on arrival or within the first day. You will **typically** need your **passport**; some shops may ask for additional details depending on the operator.

Airport kiosks are convenient but may offer fewer plan choices than city stores. Major operators in Armenia include names such as **Ucom**, **VivaCell-MTS**, and **Team**—**confirm current plans and registration rules at the point of sale**.

For a fuller first-day checklist (cash, transport, apps), see [Day one](/newcomer/day-one) and [Essential apps](/daily-life/essential-apps).
