From **Zvartnots International Airport**, most people reach central **Yerevan** by **taxi**, **ride-hail app**, or occasionally a **shuttle or pre-booked transfer**. There is no single “always best” option—**price, luggage, and time of day** matter.

Practical tips:

- Save your **destination address** (and ideally a map pin) before you land.
- **Agree or confirm the fare** when possible before the car leaves, especially for street taxis.
- Keep some **AMD cash** in case a card or app payment fails.

Step-by-step orientation, operator names, and what to watch for are in the full guide: [Airport to city](/newcomer/airport-to-city).
